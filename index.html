KWIPS: Kitchener-Waterloo Infosec and Privacy Salon

Description

KWIPS, Kitchener-Waterloo Infosec and Privacy Salon, is a privacy and computer security group that is organized by Straithe. Each of our monthly meetings starts with a 30 to 45 minute presentation from a previous attendee (or invited guest) who has volunteered to speak. Speakers present on an open access academic paper (that is not their own work!) that is privacy or computer security related. There is an hour+ after each presentation for attendees to socialize and meet other folks interested in security, privacy, and related topics.

Volunteering to Present

Any previous KWIPS attendee can volunteer to present at KWIPS. If you want to volunteer to present, please send an email to Straithe at b@straithe.com and include:

  • your name,
  • title of the paper, and
  • link to the paper.

Once your email is received, the paper is reviewed by Straithe. If your chosen paper is a fit for KWIPS, Straithe will reach out to book you in. Suggested papers are expected to be:

  • open access,
  • privacy or security focused, and
  • not the presenter's own work.

A couple of places you can find open access papers to present are https://petsymposium.org/popets/ and https://www.usenix.org/publications/proceedings.
