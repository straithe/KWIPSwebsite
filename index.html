KWIPS: Kitchener-Waterloo Infosec and Privacy Salon

KWIPS, Kitchener-Waterloo Infosec and Privacy Salon, is a privacy and computer security group that is organized by Straithe and based in Kitchener-Waterloo. Each of our monthly meetings starts with a 30 to 45 minute presentation from a previous attendee (or invited guest) who has volunteered to speak. Speakers present on an open access academic paper (that is not their own work!) that is relevant to our groups’ interests of privacy and security. There is an hour+ after each presentation for attendees to socialize and meet other like-minded folks.

Volunteering to Present

Any previous KWIPS attendee can email Straithe at b@straithe.com to volunteer to present. In your email please include your name, the title of the paper you want to present, and a link to the paper. Once your email is received, the paper is reviewed by Straithe, and, if your paper is a fit for KWIPS, Straithe will reach out to book you in. A couple of places you can find open access papers to present are https://petsymposium.org/popets/ and https://www.usenix.org/publications/proceedings.
